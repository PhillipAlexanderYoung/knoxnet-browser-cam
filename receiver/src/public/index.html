Remote Camera Setup (WireGuard)

This guided setup generates configs and commands. It does not run privileged install, firewall, or service commands for you.

Step 1 · Understand the risk

WireGuard creates a private VPN so your iPhone can reach this receiver at a VPN address from outside the LAN. Anyone with the peer config can join until you remove or rotate that key, so share configs only with trusted phones.
